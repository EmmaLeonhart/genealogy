Märta Ulfsdotter Hjortufvud

  • Daughter of Ulf Gudmarsson (Ulvåsa) and Heliga Birgitta Birgersdotter
  • Birth: 1320 in Ulvaas, Östergotlands, Sweden
  • Marta was a Swedish court lady. As a court mistress, she was responsible for Queen Margaret's upbringing and teaching.
  • Death: circa 1371 in exile in Norway.
  • Märta was read in Anteckningar om Svenska Quinnor ca 1886: "Märtha Hjorthufvud was the daughter of Nerike's lawyer Ulf Gudmarsson Hjorthufvud and S: ta Brigitta, was born around 1320 and married for the first time to Sigvid Ribbing. After Ribbing's death married Knut Algotsson . the Nordic Semiramis), she raised this and her own daughter Ingegerd (who later became the first abbess in Wadstena) so strictly according to the old way, that the rice was thereby diligently used. History tells us that she maintained such superiority over her royal pupil that, even after she became queen of Norway, she had to submit to the same crude punishment ".

Married

  • Married: Sigvid Ribbing, i Skane, Sigvid Ribbing married Märta Ulfsdotter Hjorthufvud in 1341 in på Ulfåsa herrgård i Östergötland. Märta married Sigvard Ribbing in 1337 - "the wedding at Ulvåsa" (the wedding march of the same name was composed by August Söderman much later). Mother Birgitta opposed the marriage because she disliked Sigvard Ribbing. The couple settled at Vapnö Castle outside Halmstad in Halland. Märta became a court mistress and supervisor for Margareta Valdemarsdotter, when she as queen and wife of Håkan (Håkon) VI arrived in Norway and Akershus. Märta had a daughter, Ingegerd, who was raised together with Queen Margareta Valdemarsdotter. Ingegerd became an adult abbess in Vadstena monastery, but was removed from office, accused of forgery, embezzlement and frivolous conduct. Märta was also married to the knight Knut Algotsson. Daughter of Ulf Gudmarsson Hjortufvud eller Folkunge & Brigitta Brigersdotter eller Santca Brita who had also married to the knight Knut Algotsson (lion, Bengt Algotsson's family ).
  • Married (2) After the death of her first husband in 1345, she married Knut Algotsson (Bengt Hafridsson's family) .

Sigvid "The Robber" Bengtsson Ribbing

Mentioned in 1295 as Seved Knutsson, was courtier over Halland and lord of the castle to Falkenberg. Ribbing married in 1337 with Märta Ulfsdotter (Ulvåsaätten), daughter of Saint Birgitta. The marriage has been named Bröllopet på Ulvåsa by posterity. died about 1350. Lives in Vapnö castle, Halmstad. From the family Ribbing no. 15. Sigvid was born in the early 14th century. Through an inheritance in 1340, he received the estate Vapnö in Halland. (Vapnö has belonged to the Ribbing family since the beginning of the 14th century). Sigvid was a courtier over southern Halland between the years 1330 and 1345. He is described as a cruel and hard man. Stayed in Norway and Sweden in 1335. Sigvid married Märta Ulfsdotter in 1341. She was the daughter of Ulf Gudmarsson Hjorthufvud and Birgitta Birgersdotter (Saint Birgitta). Märta was only 13 years old when it was decided that she would become the wife of Sigvid Ribbing. The marriage was more of a "political agreement" between her father and the reigning King Magnus Eriksson than a happy marriage. Sigvid Ribbing who was a "robber" made himself rich through thefts from the ships in the Kattegat between Denmark and Sweden.

King Magnus Eriksson wanted power over trade in Sweden and in order to have a calm trade between the countries, he made a well-planned move by letting Councilor Ulf Gudmarsson's daughter Märta marry Sigvid Ribbing and persuade him to stay calm.

Through the marriage between Märta and Sigvid, Ulf Gudmarsson also strengthened his position as an adviser to the king because the agreement with Sigvid Ribbing was a political success for King Magnus Eriksson.
Marta's mother, Saint Birgitta, opposed the marriage between Märta and Sigvid.
When Sigvid and Märta got married, Saint Birgitta did not want to attend the wedding at Ulfåsa but sat outside the wedding hall. After a while she regretted it and eventually she went to them in the hall at Ulfåsa. (More about this can be read in Magdalena Ribbing's book - The Ribbing 700 years in Sweden's history).
Märta and Sigvid were married for just over 10 years and had four children.
Sigvid Ribbing died in the early 1350s when the Great Depression ravaged Sweden.

Elgenstiern

born 1319 in Ulvåsa, Östergötland, died in 1399. Märta Ulfsdotter was born on Ulvåsa farm in Östergötland. Her father was teammate Ulf Gudmarsson Deer head and the mother was Heliga Birgitta. Märta was their first child. Märta was first married to Sigvid Ribbing. They married in 1341 at Ulfåsa manor in Östergötland. They were married for over 10 years and had three children together. Sigvid Ribbing died about 1350 in digerd death. Märta remarried the knight and the great man Knut Algotsson. He did not agree with the Swedish king Magnus Eriksson. In 1357 Knut Algotsson expelled from Sweden to Norway. During her stay in Norway, Märta became the mistress of Queen Margareta Valdemarsdotter. (Queen Margareta was born in Denmark in 1353. At the age of 10, Margareta married the 13-year-old Håkon of Norway. Håkon was the son of the Swedish king Magnus Eriksson. Queen Margareta remained in Denmark until 1366 and moved to Håkon in Akershus in Norway where Märta raised Queen Margaret). Märta and Ulf's daughter Ingegerd later became abbesses in Vadstena monastery, but were dismissed from the office when she was accused of forgery and embezzlement. Märta died in 1399.

Märta Ulfsdotter (Ulvåsa)

Heliga Birgittas dotter.

född omkring 1319, död 1371, var en svensk hovdam. Hon var som hovmästarinna ansvarig för drottning Margaretas uppfostran och undervisning.

Märta var dotter till Ulf Gudmarsson till Ulvåsa och Heliga Birgitta. Hon gifte sig med Sigvid Ribbing, hövitsman över södra Halland. Hennes första bröllop ägde rum år 1337 och har blivit berömt som Bröllopet på Ulvåsa. Detta finns omnämnt i sägnen om Heliga Birgittas helgonhistoria. Birgitta ogillade Märtas förste make och kallade honom rövare. Efter sin första makes död 1345 gifte hon om sig med Knut Algotsson (Bengt Hafridssons ätt).

Märta valdes år 1366 till hovmästarinna åt den framtida drottning Margareta, då Margareta bosatte sig i Norge. Hon undervisade sin dotter Ingegerd tillsammans med Margareta, och ska ha använt sig flitigt av aga som undervisningsmetod. Enligt legenden ska hon ha bibehållit: "en sådan överlägsenhet över sin kungliga elev, att denna, till och med sedan hon blivit drottning i Norige, måst underkasta sig samma råa bestraffning."
