Why raw frequency is useless, and what replaces it Every chain starts at the same person, so her father, grandfather and their line appear in almost every path and top any plain count. They are not connectors — they are the only road out. The separator is depth spread: how many different step-depths a person is reached at. Always reached at the same depth means one route in. Reached at several depths means several branches arrive there, and that is what a connector is.
