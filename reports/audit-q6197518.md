# Audit — why `Q6197518`'s `mul` label became an English-only one

**Emma, 2026-08-30:** *"add to the end of the queue an audit of what happened here on this one
https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q6197518 the multi language label was 'corrected' to one that was
English only and I do not understand why"*.

**It was us.** `reports/wikidata-touched-not-created.qs` did it, and the file's own header states
the invariant that broke.

## What happened, from the revision history

| when (UTC) | who | what |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-08-28 03:03 | 日巫女 | `wbsetlabel-add:1\|mul` → **Svantepolk Knutsson** — her own hand edit |
| 2026-08-30 19:47:34 | 日巫女, `#quickstatements` | `wbsetaliases-add:1\|mul` → *Svantepolk Knutsson* |
| 2026-08-30 19:47:45 | 日巫女, `#quickstatements` | `wbsetlabel-set:1\|mul` → **Svantepolk of Viby** |
| 2026-08-30 19:57:48 | 日巫女 | `wbsetlabel-set:1\|mul` → **Svantepolk Knutsson** — her reverting it ten minutes later |

Both 19:47 edits are one QuickStatements run, `#temporary_batch_1788119173098`.

The line that did it is in `reports/wikidata-touched-not-created.qs`:

    #   Q6197518: set the mul label to "Svantepolk of Viby" (it had none)   # NOTE: this en label carries a title, not just a name
    Q6197518	Lmul	"Svantepolk of Viby"

## Two separate defects, and the second is the one that matters

**1. The premise `(it had none)` was false, and the file promises it is not.** Its header reads:

> *Every edit here is ADDITIVE. A mul label is only set where the item has none, so nothing she
> may have written by hand is overwritten -- CLAUDE.md: a label REPLACES.*

She set that `mul` on **2026-08-28 03:03**. The batch ran on **08-30 19:47** believing the slot
empty, so it was built against a snapshot at least two days stale. This is `CLAUDE.md` § *Emma
edits the tree and the items BY HAND, continuously. Snapshots go stale in minutes*, and the
alias-first preservation step is what limited the damage: *Svantepolk Knutsson* survived as an
`Amul` because the batch wrote the alias one second before overwriting the label.

**2. The value was the English label, and the generator knew.** `Svantepolk of Viby` is the `en`
label. `mul` is the language-neutral slot and carries the native form, which is what
`Svantepolk Knutsson` is. The generator emitted a trailing `# NOTE: this en label carries a
title, not just a name` on this very line — so the check existed, fired, and did not stop the
emission. A warning that does not gate anything is not a guard.

## Three more of the same, checked live 2026-08-31

The same block holds four `Lmul` lines the generator flagged with that NOTE. Live state now:

| `Q101247444` | Ingegerd Svantepolksdotter | Ingegerd Svantepolksdotter of Viby, heiress, lady of Händelöö | the long `en` form — a **regression** |
| `Q274606` | *none* | Berengar I of Italy | Berengar I of Italy |
| `Q3743799` | *none* | Canute, Duke of Estonia | Canute, Duke of Estonia |
| `Q6197518` | Svantepolk Knutsson | Svantepolk of Viby | would clobber her fix again |

So two of them (`Q274606`, `Q3743799`) had **not** run yet and would have put an English title
into `mul` on the next run; one would have undone her correction; one would have replaced a clean
label with a description. All four lines are removed.

The remaining `Lmul` lines in that block — `Gisela`, `Arne Garborg`, `Helena Guttormsdatter`,
`Knut Algotsson` — are plain names and are left alone.

## What is *not* fixed here

`Q274606` and `Q3743799` still carry no `mul` at all. Dropping the line leaves them as they were
rather than asserting an English label, which is the safer of the two — but the right `mul` for
each is a native form with the title stripped and the regnal ordinal handled as `P7338`, and
that is a naming decision rather than a string edit. Left for the label work.
