# Where the 26 relationship paths actually break

Measured 2026-08-06 against `out/merged.ged` (99 exports, **203 323**
individuals). Every row is an exact join on the Geni profile ID; the
name-matching fallback is not used anywhere in this report, because all 3 464
steps across all 26 paths carry an ID.

This answers Emma's question — *"for the added pages did you get the present and
absent profiles?"* — and tests her reading of why the remote-people hypothesis
is not paying.

**The four new exports of this evening are NOT in this measurement.**
`out/merged.ged` predates them. That is deliberate: it makes everything below a
*baseline*, and § The prediction records what should change when the merge is
re-run.

**No path ends in a gap.** Every one of the 21 absent runs has a resume point;

frontiers — the tree already reaches the remote figure and is missing people in
the middle.

## Emma's reading is supported, and the shape is sharper than "a few individuals"

> *"they are going through relatively sparse ancient networks with most links
> coming through a few critical individuals, vs more small-worlded modern day
> data … we are likely already experiencing strong diminishing returns for
> ancient figures."*

Three things in the data say so.

**1. Five paths break at the identical ten people.** `daughter-of-the-king-of-assyria`,
`eleazar-ii-samaritan-high-priest`, `nn-daughter-of-berenice`, `nn-of-malwa` and
`nn` have five different endpoints and break at **steps 35–44 in every case** —
same doorway (Gisela of Friuli `6000000008592343633`), same first absent person
(Berengar I, her father), same resume (Leutharis II, duke of the Alemannians).
Ten people, fifty step-slots, one bridge. This is the Alemannian/Carolingian
line already queued as item 2.5, now confirmed as a shared articulation point
rather than five separate problems.

**2. It repeats independently in the Arabian material.** `scorpion-i` and
`pasuti` break at steps 175–193 — same doorway ('A'idhullah al-'Ashiri
`6000000226741965864`), same 19 people, same resume (Ra'la Al-Jurhumi). The
second run of `psamtik-ii` resumes at that *same* Ra'la Al-Jurhumi. Nineteen
Jurhumid/Qahtani people carry two or three paths each.

**3. Concentration, counted.** 265 absent slots over 196 distinct people, but 29
people account for 98 of the slots. Two clusters — the Alemannian ten and the
Jurhumid/Qahtani nineteen — are ~98 slots between them, about **37% of every
gap in the corpus of paths**.

That is the mechanism Emma named: a sparse ancient graph whose connectivity runs
through a small number of individuals, so one missing bridge shows up as many
broken paths and inflates the apparent size of the problem.

### The diminishing returns are visible, not just plausible

Eleven of 26 paths are complete. Among the pages saved today, **`gong-liu` holds
249 of 249** — a 249-step chain into ancient China with nothing missing at all.
`makeda-to-enlil-nirari` (225/225) and `makeda-to-matthew` (219/219) were
already the same story on 2026-08-06 morning, and queue item 0.0 flagged the
risk then. Saving more remote-people pages is now returning chains this tree
already holds.

**The instrument measures our tree's shape, not Geni's gaps** — which is exactly
what item 0.0 said the honest conclusion would be if several came back complete.
Several have.

### What does *not* follow

The long paths are not evidence of a small-world modern network by contrast,
because nothing here measured a modern chain. All 26 paths start at Makeda Queen
of Sheba and run into antiquity. The ancient-vs-modern comparison is **stated as
a hypothesis and is untested here**; § A test that needs no export gives the way
to test it.

| 2 | 'A'idhullah al-'Ashiri `6000000226741965864` | **~48 slots** (19 people, 2–3 paths each) | `scorpion-i`, `pasuti`, `psamtik-ii` |
| 3 | 蘇瑗 `6000000129573950864` | 52 slots | 1 path |
| 4 | 張穆之 `6000000010476150707` | 27 slots | 1 path |
| 5 | 黄若 `6000000007357108232` | 25 slots | 1 path |

Ranks 1 and 2 are the ones the concentration argument favours: the same effort
buys several paths at once. Ranks 3–5 are long but private to one chain each —
and a 52-person run is far past the 6–9 steps a targeted export has actually
been observed to span here, so `zeng-yuan` is at least two exports and possibly
not worth them.

## A test that needs no export

Emma's ancient-sparse / modern-small-world claim can be tested against data
already on disk, and should be, because it is currently an interpretation rather
than a measurement:

**For every person on all 26 paths, count their edges (parents + children +
spouses) in the merged tree, and plot degree against step index.** The claim
predicts degree ≈ 2 through the ancient stretches — a chain — rising towards the
modern end. The Hata neighbourhood walk already found exactly that shape locally
(41 people, "a father-to-son thread with two clan exits and a single marriage"),
so this generalises a result rather than guessing.

A second, sharper one: **count how many of the 26 paths share each absent
person.** Done above (29 people carry 98 slots), but the same statistic computed
over *held* people would say whether the sharing is a property of the gaps or of
the whole graph. If held people are equally shared, the "critical individuals"
finding is just what any chain-shaped graph looks like and carries no extra
information.

Neither was run tonight — both want a pass over the merge with family edges
parsed, and this session is under an instruction to keep CPU down.
