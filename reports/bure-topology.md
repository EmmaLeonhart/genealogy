# The Bure cluster: what is actually disconnected

Generated by `scripts/link-bure-people.py`. Emma, 2026-08-25: *"What is the topology of them? Like of the bure people what percentage of the wikidata linked ones are just directly connected through geni even though they are absent on wikidata?"*

**The short answer is that they are not missing links to each other. They are missing the people in between.**

## Direct relationships

Between two Bure people who both hold a QID, Geni records **4,478** direct relationships. Wikidata already states **3,857** of them — **86.1%**. Only **588** are missing, and they are in `reports/wikidata-bure-links.qs`.

So the premise that these people are unlinked *to each other* does not hold where we can see both ends. Wikidata has almost all of it.

## Where the disconnection really is

**Pairs joined on Geni only through people who have no Wikidata item:**

| hops | pairs | people to create |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 546 | 1 |
| 3 | 546 | 2 |
| 4 | 679 | 3 |

**546 of those pairs are one person apart, and only 233 distinct people account for all of them.** Creating those 233 closes 546 joins at once — the single densest one closes 22.

`reports/bure-bridges.tsv` lists every pair with the people in between.

## The number that bounds everything above

**113 of the 1706 Bure profiles carrying a Geni id are not in our corpus at all** — no export we hold reaches them. Their relationships are invisible to this measurement, so every figure here is a **floor**. More exports would find more edges; none of these numbers can go down.
