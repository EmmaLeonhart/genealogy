# The 33 Samaritan high priests: why the component cannot be joined

**Conclusion: it cannot, and the blocking half is on our side of the gap.**
Emma's reading — that the change in the priestly house makes this line genuinely
unconnectable — holds, and this is the measurement behind it.

## What the component is

192 exports, 381,035 people, **2 connected components**: 381,002 and **33**.
The 33 are the Samaritan high-priestly line — Abisha, Phinehas, Elazar, Tsedaka,
Aabed-El ben Asher, Yaacob ben Uzzi, Yoseph ben Ab-Hisda and the rest.

**It is one descent with one door.** Of the 33, exactly one has no parent
recorded: **Tabia ha'Åbtå'i** `6000000220294810877`, with all 32 others beneath
him across 11 generations. His Geni record carries no dates, no place, no
occupation — a name, a sex, and one child, Tsedaka II ben Tabia. So there is a
single attachment point, not 33 candidate doorways.

## What the sources say above him

**1624 is the break.** Shalma I ben Phinehas, the last Samaritan High Priest of
the line of **Eleazar son of Aaron**, died without male succession. The office
passed to a family claiming descent from Aaron's other son **Ithamar** — the
*Åbtå* order, and its first high priest was **Tsedaka II ben Tabia ha'Åbtå'i**,
the 113th, our Tabia's son. Every Samaritan High Priest since 1624 descends from
him. The line nearly ended again in 1787, when a four-year-old, Shalma ben Tabia,
was the only survivor; the present families all descend from that child.

**The Ithamar descent is asserted, not enumerated.** Wikipedia's article on the
Samaritan High Priest states the Åbtå family traces "back to Itamar, the brother
of Elazar" and gives **no intermediate ancestors** — no father for Tabia, no
chain between him and Ithamar. That is roughly three thousand years and on the
order of a hundred generations that no source enumerates.

**The chronicle that would carry it does not.** The *Tolidah*, the oldest
Samaritan historical work, compiled 1149 by Eleazar ben 'Amram of Nablus,
continued by Jacob ben Ishmael in 1346 and by later scribes to 1859, gives the
dynasty of high priests — but the dynasty it records is **the Eleazar line**, the
one that ended in 1624. The incoming Åbtå family's own ascent is exactly the part
the chronicle tradition does not cover, because they were not the high-priestly
house while it was being written.

So the upward link is a **claim of descent from Ithamar**, not a genealogy. There
is nothing to import even in principle.

## And the other side of the gap is empty

This is the part that settles it regardless of what any Samaritan source turns
out to hold. **The corpus contains no biblical line at all.** Searched across all
192 GEDCOMs by name:

| looked for | found in corpus |
| --- | ---: |
| Ithamar son of Aaron | **0** |
| Aaron the High Priest | **0** |
| Eleazar son of Aaron | **0** |
| Moses | **0** |
| Levi son of Jacob | **0** |
| Jacob / Israel the patriarch | **0** |
| Abraham the patriarch | **0** |
| Adam | **0** |
| King David | **0** |

Every apparent hit is a modern person carrying the given name — Adam Buchmann,
Noah Grant, Levi Jacob Israel Sachs. The tree reaches Woden, Fjolnir and Jimmu,
so it is not that legendary ancestry is absent in general; the **biblical**
spine specifically is not in it.

Which means the join would need *both* an enumerated Åbtå→Ithamar chain that does
not exist **and** an import of Aaron, Levi and the patriarchs into the main
component to attach it to. Neither exists today, and only the second is something
an export could ever supply.

## What would change this

One thing, and it is cheap: a `Forest` export seeded on **Tabia ha'Åbtå'i**
`6000000220294810877`. `Forest` follows spouse links as well as blood ones, so a
`Forest` ball that comes back with no parent has **found none to follow** — the
same asymmetry that settled the Hata question in `reports/hata.md`. That converts
"Geni probably records nothing above him" from inference into measurement.

It will not join the component even if it succeeds, because of the empty column
above. It would only tell us how far up Geni goes before it stops.

**Agentic RAG over outside sources is not the missing piece.** The gap is not
that the sources are hard to find; it is that the genealogy they would supply was
never written, and that the tree has no biblical anchor to attach it to.
