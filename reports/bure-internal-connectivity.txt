READING, 2026-08-28.  Emma's hypothesis was that the family is "well connected
outside itself but not within itself", which would explain why 5000-person

What actually defeats the balls is SPAN, not looseness.  Category:Bureatten is
a DESCENT category covering roughly 400 years and a dozen generations.  A
5000-person breadth-first ball reaches four or five generations in each
direction from its seed and cannot hold both ends of a twelve-generation
descent.  So each export closes the sub-cluster it lands in and no more -- the
Knos trio in one ball, the Sandels trio in another, Ehrenstrahle plus von
Hartmansdorff in a third.  That is the observed pattern all day.

The consequence for the campaign is that coverage is reached by seeding in
several places along the descent, not by finding one well-placed seed.  The
tail of 30 people at 4+ hops and 11 effectively disconnected is where the
remaining work is.
