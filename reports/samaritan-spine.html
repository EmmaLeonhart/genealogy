The Samaritan Itamar line

Itamar ben Aaron down to Tabia ha'Abta'i, generation by generation — a view of gedcom/samaritan-itamar-spine.ged, the file built for entering into Geni.

Five people here are named by a source. The other 115 are positions, not people. They are labelled “4th generation Samaritan Itamar line” and so on because that is exactly what the record asserts — a place in a named lineage. It is not a personal name and no name has been invented anywhere.

The length of the unnamed stretch is borrowed from the parallel Phinhas line, which the source gives as 112 generations father-to-son from Aaron to 1624. Nobody counted this line. Do not read these numbers as a measurement of it.

Generation 110 is weaker still: the source does not state the distance between 'Abed Ela ben Shalma and Yusef, and it may be zero.

    'Abed Ela ben Shalma
    President of the House of 'Abtah
    priest, poet, translator
    Forefather of the current Israelite Samaritan priestly families. Born and active in Damascus, then moved to Nablus to serve the high priests. Great poet, translator and teacher of religion. 'Abtah means Translator (Arabic: Haftawi) - the Itamar priests rendered the Phinhas High Priest's Hebrew reading of the Pentateuch into Aramaic, and are named for that duty. Source: A.B. - The Samaritan News / The Samaritan Update, March-April 2012, 'The High Priesthood and the Israelite Samaritan Priests', by Benyamim Tsedaka
