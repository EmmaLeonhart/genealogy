# What each kind of zipper evidence is actually worth

Generated by `scripts/measure-zipper-reliability.py`. **Nothing here is reasoned from what sounds right.** Emma, 2026-08-25: *"a lot of these rules are empirical and we need to empirically study our data to figure out what to make of it. Don't jump to conclusions based on what sounds like it might be true. Even parents isn't certain."*

Two independent checks, neither of which asks Wikidata anything: whether the two birth years are more than ten years apart, and whether a source that is **not** the zipper puts the same pair together or pulls it apart.

`date` selects on the birth year, so its date column is circular by construction and is marked `--`. Read it against `solo` and `name`, not alongside them.
