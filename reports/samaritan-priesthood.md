# The Samaritan priestly succession, from the community's own record

**Source: `A.B. — The Samaritan News` / The Samaritan Update, March–April 2012,
"The High Priesthood and the Israelite Samaritan Priests", by Benyamim Tsedaka.**
Transcribed 2026-08-13 from the saved page.

This answers the question `reports/samaritan-component.md` left open. That report
said no source named a father for Tabia ha'Åbtå'i and no chain existed above him.
**Both are wrong**, and this is the correction.

---

## Tabia's father is Yusef

> "they asked the priest **Tsedaka b. Tabia b. Yusef** to be the first High Priest
> from the family of Itamar to head the Israelite Samaritans."

Our component's root, `Tabia ha'Åbtå'i` `6000000220294810877`, is **Tabia b.
Yusef**. His son Tsedaka became the first Itamar-line High Priest in 1624.

## And the family forefather is named, with a place and a title

> "The forefather of the current priestly families was the priest **'Abed Ela b.
> Shalma** of the top of the Samaritan Wisdom. He was born and active in
> **Damascus**, then moved to Nablus to serve the high priests. He was great
> poet, translator and teacher of religion. His title in Damascus was
> **'President of the House of 'Abtah'**."

So above Yusef the line runs into a named Damascene priest, **'Abed Ela b.
Shalma**, whose own father is given as **Shalma**.

## What 'Abtah actually means — not a place, a job

> "They helped the Phinhas High Priest in translating his Hebrew reading of the
> Pentateuch into Aramaic… Because of this special duty they were called
> **'Abtah = Translator**, or in Arabic: **Haftawi**."

`ha'Åbtå'i` is an occupational epithet — *the translator* — carried by the Itamar
priests who served as Aramaic interpreters to the Phinhas high priests. Reading
it as a toponym would be the `秦州成紀` mistake in `CLAUDE.md` § *A clan name is
not a clan*, in reverse.

## The line the Itamar priests replaced

> "In 1624 CE the last High Priest of the family of Phinhas, **Shalmaiah b.
> Phinhas b. Eleazar** died after serving 11 years in office and he left after
> him **only one daughter**."

Three named generations, a term of office, and a daughter. He disappeared en
route from Nablus to the Samaritans of Gaza; tradition said he was taken by the
Almighty, and the 17th-century poet **Marchiv b. Jacob** settled it in a letter to
Europe writing that "the last Rabban died in our time".

**The Phinhas line ran 112 generations father-to-son from Aaron b. 'Amram.** The
current High Priest is the **132nd since Aaron**. Two Damascus high priests,
**Itamar b. 'Amram** and **Yusef b. 'Azzi**, are the only Damascene ones counted
in the 112.

**Two sibling pairs are stated outright** — Matzliach and Abisha, Asher and
Phinhas — which are edges Geni's patronymics alone would not give.

**Shalma b. Tabia's gap is explained rather than implied.** His father Tabia died
in 1787 and Shalma's term begins in 1798: "Shalma was 4 years old when his father
Tabia died. He was educated by the Samaritan sages till he became 15 years old
and they found that he is qualified to be a High Priest." **So Shalma b. Tabia
was born in 1783.**

## The current High Priest's full chain, as published

> "Aaron b. Ab-Hisda b. HP Jacob b. Aaron b. HP Shalma b. HP Tabia b. Yitzhaq b.
> HP Abraham b. HP Yitzhaq b. HP Tabia b. HP **Tsedaka b. Tabia b. Yusef**."

Eleven generations, ending on our component's root and his father.

## The other households, same source

**Dinfi** — one family escaped the **1625** Damascus pogrom initiated by the
governor **Mardam Bek**, and are the forefathers of the household. Largest of the
four households; contains **Altif + Sassoni = Sirrawi**.

**Community size: 751 people on 1 January 2012**, with the 'Abtah priestly family
second largest after Dinfi.

## What this means for the disconnected component

The 33 are the 'Abtah priests. Their root now has:

- a **father**, Yusef
- a documented **family forefather**, 'Abed Ela b. Shalma of Damascus, with *his*
  father named
- an explicit descent claim to **Itamar b. Aaron**
- a 20-entry succession with exact dates, two sibling edges, and one derived
  birth year (Shalma b. Tabia, 1783)

None of that is in Geni. It is **material to create**, which is what this project
is for — not material to search for. `reports/samaritan-component.md`'s conclusion
that the line was undocumented is superseded by this file.
