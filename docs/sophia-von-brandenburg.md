# Sophia von Brandenburg (1450–1480) and the Underberge family: 2026-10-09

**Why she matters:** she is the one dead end among Emma's ancestors whose name points at Abul
Hamza's descendants (`dead-end-candidates.md`). In the synoptic tree she is 16 generations above
Emma, has no parents, and is married to Gard Toreson Underberge II (b. 1454); their sons are Halvor
(1475), Tore (1481) and Osmund (1485) Gardson Underberge. If she was a Brandenburg (Hohenzollern)
princess, she is the link.

## What was found

- **No source connects a Sophia von Brandenburg to Underberge.** Web searches in English, German
  and Norwegian found only other Sophias of Brandenburg (Brandenburg-Stendal c. 1300–1356, the
  Ansbach and Kulmbach Sophias of the 1600s–1700s, Queen Sophie Magdalene of Denmark-Norway), none
  married into Norway in the 1400s.
- **None of Albrecht III Achilles' 17 children in the synoptic tree is a Sophia**, and no other
  Hohenzollern of the right age in the tree is named as her parent.
- **Underberge is a real farm and family:** Under-Berge in Høgsfjord (Høle, now Sandnes,
  Rogaland), seat of the "Berge-ætta". The published work on that family is Sigleif Engen,
  "Berge-ætta fra Høgsfjord i Ryfylke", *Lagnaden* 1942 (pp. 40–142), and Eyvin Dahl, "Gard
  Toresons hustru: Ramborg Knutsdotter", *Lagnaden* 1946 (pp. 13–46). Those concern Gard Toreson
  (lagmann in Ryfylke, d. c. 1427) and Ramborg Knutsdotter, said to be a natural daughter of Knut
  Algotsson; their son Tore Gardson is called the ancestor of the Under-Berge family. Gard was the
  main farmer at Under-Berge in 1519 and 1521 (a later Gard). Nothing found names a Brandenburg
  wife in that family.

## Reading

A Brandenburg princess marrying a Rogaland farmer in the 1470s would be recorded somewhere on the
Hohenzollern side, and nothing is. The most likely reading is that "von Brandenburg" on this
profile is a family tradition or a genealogy-site attribution without a source, not a documented
princess. That is a reading, not a finding: the *Lagnaden* articles were not seen in full, and
the original Geni profile's notes are still to be read from the exports on disk.

Whether to attach her to the Hohenzollerns anyway is Emma's call (where a line attaches in the
story). Nothing has been applied anywhere.
