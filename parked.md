# Parked

Things Emma has looked at and deliberately set aside — **not** blocked, not
forgotten, and not to be quietly resolved by whoever touches the code next. A
parked item may turn out to be useful; what is parked is the *decision*, because
the information needed to make it is not in hand.

Emma asked for this list by name on 2026-08-11, having used the word
deliberately once before (`NAME` language marking, below).

Format: what it is, who parked it and when, and what it would take to unpark.

**This list is currently empty, and that is the point of it.** Every item put
here on 2026-08-11 was resolved the same day by asking Emma directly rather than
letting it sit. Her instruction: *"ask a user question on every single fucking
thing that is so-called parked… Ask me for anything that's so-called blocked on
user action, whether I'm actually going to do it or not, because it's most likely
I'm not going to do it."*

So the rule for this file: **nothing enters it without Emma having been asked
first and having chosen to park it.** "Parked" is her word about a decision she
declined to make, never a place to put a question I did not ask.
