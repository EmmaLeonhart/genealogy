# The Inbe (忌部 / 斎部) clan: what is known

## The question (as the user put it)

Who were the Inbe, where did they come from, how did they spread, and can
their people be traced back, as a GEDCOM genealogy, to the gods, via Wikidata
or FamilySearch? Why do they feel so much less "real" than the Nakatomi, whose
genealogy is well defined? See `INTENT.md` for the user's own words.

## Current answer (early, 2026-10-02)

**Wikidata cannot get a real Inbe to the gods through Futodama.** It has the
god layer (Futodama, his father Takamimusubi, his children and grandson Ame no
Tomi) and about a dozen historical Inbe, but none of the historical people has
a parent. The two layers never meet. Confidence: high for what Wikidata
contains as of today.

**The branch ancestors are on different divine lines.** Even in the myth layer,
Ame no Hiwashi (Awa) descends from Takamimusubi via Omoikane, and Ama no
Mahitotsu (Tsukushi/Ise) descends from Susanoo. So "not all blood relatives"
holds in the myths themselves, not only in history. Confidence: medium (Wikidata
parentage, to check against the Kogo Shūi and Shinsen Shōjiroku).

**The one Wikidata path to the gods goes through the Oda's claimed Taira
descent**, not through the Inbe. Chikazane, an Inbe called the Oda ancestor, is
given the Taira as his father's line. Details are in
`notes/wikidata-survey.md`.
