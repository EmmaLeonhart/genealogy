What Hethel has that the synoptic tree does not

Richard Borsheim’s ancestors in Hethel Pedigree.ged, tested against the synoptic tree on the edge, not the name. A person counts as present only when some synoptic person shares two distinctive name tokens and at least one token with a parent or child. A matched string alone is not enough — that test called Richard himself missing.

The Pfinzing descent breaks in five people

Both ends are already in our tree — 73 Underberge, 75 Tenga, 77 Pfinzing. What is missing is the join.

Tore Toreson III Underberge  →  Ragnhild Eyvindsdtr Rømer (gen 17)  →  Eivind Ogmundson Rømer (18) … Margaretha Geuschmidt (22)  →  Reichsschultheiss Heinrich Geuschmidt (23)  →  Rapato Geuschmidt (24)  →  Pfinzing von Henfenfeld

Geuschmidt does not exist in the synoptic tree at all — zero people. It is the connector between the Rogaland farm lines and the Nuremberg patriciate, and it is the whole reason the descent is not on our side.
