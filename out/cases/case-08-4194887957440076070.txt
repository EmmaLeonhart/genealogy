2 CONT The Plantagenet family name was originally just a nickname for Geoffrey. He many times wore a sprig with yellow flowers in his hat.  The flower was named "genet" or "genistae" in the French of the tim
2 CONC es--thus his nickname was "Plant-a-Genet".  Genet was supposedly a traditional flower of the Anjou family dating back to the time of Fulk, The Great, Count of Anjou 898-941 who was scourged (in order 
2 CONC to atone for past sins) with broom twigs of the Genet while on pilgrimage in Jerusalem.   Most people of the times had personal nicknames such as "Beauclerc",  "Curtmantel", "Longshanks", and "Lacklan
2 CONC d", but Geoffrey's stuck and eventually (many generations later) became the family name.  Geoffrey's immediate descendants were probably not known as the Plantagenet family at the time they lived, it 
2 CONC was only later that the Plantagenet family name was applied to all descendants of Geoffrey.
