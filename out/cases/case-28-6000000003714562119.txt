1 NOTE Louis VI, called The Fat (1081-1137), king of France (1108-37), son and 
2 CONT successor of Philip I; he was married to Adelaide of Savoy. Almost his 
2 CONT entire reign was spent in subduing the robber barons, who preyed on the 
2 CONT environs of Paris but were finally forced to yield to royal authority. For 
2 CONT some 20 years during the period from 1109-1135, Louis waged war against 
2 CONT Henry I, the Norman king of England, and against Henry's son-in-law, Holy 
2 CONT Roman Emperor Henry V; he successfully repelled an invasion by Henry V in 
2 CONT 1124. Louis greatly strengthed the royal power in France, granted 
2 CONT benefactions to the church and privileges to towns, and became known as 
2 CONT the protector of the peasants and as a fearless military leader. He was 
2 CONT succeeded on the throne by his son Louis VII.
